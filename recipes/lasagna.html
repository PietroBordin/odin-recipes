lasagnaricetta

Description

A traditional Italian pasta dish with basil pesto.

Ingredients

  • 200g pasta
  • 50g basil pesto
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Bring water to a boil.
  2. Add salt and cook the pasta.
  3. Drain the pasta.
  4. Mix the pasta with pesto.
  5. Serve with Parmesan cheese.
